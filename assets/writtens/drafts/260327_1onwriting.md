# On writing

At random times throughout the past years, I've had ideas or thoughts that I thought, " I'd want to write about that". Just because thinking and hypotheticalling and philosphizing is fun and I wish it didn't have to stay in my own head.

But as I sit down and try to write anything, I get blocked up by a certain feeling. I'm so afraid of coming off as "knowing shit". 

Something I've always struggled with is the push and pull of ego. On one side I beleive I like who I am, that what I've grown into and what I've curated of myself is something I'm proud of; I'd want to share that with other people. But this is egotistical; do I think the person I've become is interesting enough to make a whole website of myself? It seems self absorbed to have such a main character way of thinking of myself. I'm quite uncomfortable with 



is so much more cool or interesting than anyone else? I'm not nessesarily a main character so I shouldn't frame my life as if I'm getting crazy character development. Thus I don't think I should assert myself as unique to others. I'm afraid of putting myself into the world in a way that demands attention of myself. Am I, or anyone, 'allowed' to do something that does nothing but put attention onto myself, even if it's soemthing about myself that I'm proud of? Why do I feel that I am interesting enough to make a whole website about myself.

So when I sit down and try to write anything that I think, I get nagged by the fear that I'm going to write in a way that reveals self absorbance, that I know what I'm talking about and you don't.

If I knew the answer to this issue I would have worded one side as much more unreasonable than the other.





 I get a certain uneasy feeling when I see certain people I know in real life be influencers; the way they draw attention and make themselves .. seems like a type of ego I have internalized I'm not allowed to do.
